    <h2>Hướng Dẫn Sử Dụng BST Twin Blade Boring Tool</h2>
    
    <div class="guide-section">
      <h3>1️⃣ Setup & Installation BST Twin Blade Boring</h3>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Extension Bar Selection:</strong> Chọn extension bar length phù hợp với hole depth: 70-150mm cho shallow, 150-250mm cho medium, 250-400mm cho deep hole. <strong>BST twin blade boring</strong> với L/D ratio >3:1 nên dùng carbide bar để giảm vibration.</li>
        <li><strong>Insert Installation:</strong> 2 insert TPGH/TCMT phải installed đối xứng 180° để đảm bảo balanced cutting force. Torque insert screw: 8-12 Nm for TPGH, 10-15 Nm for TCMT. Check insert seating flatness trước khi boring.</li>
        <li><strong>Tool Holder Mounting:</strong> <strong>BST twin blade boring tool</strong> mount vào RBH rough boring head hoặc direct holder. Ensure ±0.02mm runout, coolant port aligned nếu có through-coolant.</li>
      </ul>

    <div class="guide-section">
      <h3>3️⃣ Maintenance & Troubleshooting</h3>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Insert Wear Check:</strong> Inspect 2 insert edges sau mỗi 50-100 holes. Nếu 1 insert wear nhanh hơn → check unbalanced force, phải re-install insert hoặc check extension bar runout.</li>
        <li><strong>Vibration Issues:</strong> Nếu <strong>BST twin blade boring</strong> vibrate → check: 1) 2 insert đều sharp? 2) Extension bar runout <0.02mm? 3) Cutting speed quá cao? Reduce speed 20-30% nếu vibration vẫn còn.</li>
        <li><strong>Poor Surface Finish:</strong> Ra >3.2μm → check feed rate (reduce 20%), coolant flow (increase 30%), insert grade (switch to finishing grade M10-M15 for stainless).</li>
      </ul>
