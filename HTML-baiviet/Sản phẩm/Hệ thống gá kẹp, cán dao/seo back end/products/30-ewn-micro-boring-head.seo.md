1. **Lắp holder vào spindle:** BT40/50, HSK-A63/100, torque 250-350 Nm.
2. **Attach EWN to holder:** Thread connection M16/M20, torque 50 Nm.
3. **Set cutting parameters:**
   - **Vc:** 150-250 m/min (aluminum), 80-120 m/min (steel).
   - **f:** 0.05-0.15 mm/rev (finish cut).
   - **ap:** 0.1-0.3mm/pass (finish), 0.05-0.1mm (final pass).
4. **Kiểm tra runout:** Runout <0.01mm tại insert tip (critical for IT6).

### Bước 4: Điều Chỉnh Diameter Bằng Micrometer Dial

1. **Measure current hole diameter:** Bằng bore gauge/CMM, ví dụ Φ44.95mm after rough boring.
2. **Calculate adjustment needed:** Target Φ45.00mm → cần tăng 0.05mm → 25 graduations (0.05 / 0.002 = 25).
3. **Adjust micrometer dial:** Xoay dial 25 graduations clockwise (increase diameter), lock adjustment screw torque 8 Nm.
4. **Verify with test cut:** Boring 5mm depth, measure diameter, fine-tune ±1-2 graduations nếu cần.

### Bước 5: Gia Công Finish Boring

1. **Z-axis approach:** Di chuyển EWN đến start position (Z=0), clearance 2mm.
2. **X-axis feed in:** Feed vào depth of cut 0.2mm (finish pass), spindle n = 2000 rpm (Φ45mm, Vc 200 m/min aluminum).
3. **Boring cycle:** G85 cycle (feed in, rapid out), feed rate f = 0.1 mm/rev.
4. **Final pass:** Depth of cut 0.05mm, feed rate f = 0.05 mm/rev → achieve Ra 0.4μm, IT6.

### Bước 6: Kiểm Tra Tolerance & Surface Finish

1. **Measure diameter:** Bore gauge 3-point measurement, kiểm tra ±0.008mm (IT6).
2. **Check roundness:** CMM measurement, roundness <0.01mm.
3. **Verify surface finish:** Surface roughness tester, Ra <0.6μm.
4. **If out of tolerance:** Fine-tune micrometer dial ±1 graduation (±0.002mm), repeat final pass.
