## 5 Tính Năng Vượt Trội

### 1. Floating Chuck Tension-Compression Mechanism – Self-Compensating

**BT tension compression tapping holder** hoạt động với floating chuck mechanism - chuck tự do di chuyển axially (theo trục Z) trong stroke ±0.3mm. Khi taro đi xuống (cutting stroke), spring compression absorbs excess feed → tension mode. Khi taro lên (return stroke), spring extension compensates slow retract → compression mode. **BT tension compression tapping** mechanism tự động synchronize spindle rotation với Z-axis feed mà không cần CNC rigid tapping function. Result: smooth threading, no tap breakage from feed mismatch.

### 2. Overload Protection with Torque Slip Clutch – Prevents Tap Breakage

**BT tension compression tapping holder** tích hợp slip clutch mechanism với preset torque limits: G3 collet (2.26-11.3 N.m for M3-M12), G12 collet (22.6-67.8 N.m for M12-M24), G24 collet (56.5-170 N.m for M24-M42). Khi cutting torque vượt threshold → clutch automatically slips → tap stops rotating while spindle continues → prevents tap breakage. **BT tension compression tapping** protection system critical cho blind hole tapping khi tap touches bottom or hits hard spot in material.

### 3. Radial Misalignment Compensation ±0.3mm – For Cast/Drilled Holes

**BT tension compression tapping holder** floating chuck compensates radial misalignment ±0.3mm giữa tap axis và hole axis. Cast iron holes (sand casting) thường có positional tolerance ±0.2-0.5mm → floating chuck auto-centers tap into hole entrance → reduces tap walking và side loading. Aluminum die-cast holes với draft angles → floating compensates angular misalignment up to 0.5°. Result: 70% reduction in tap breakage rate cho cast holes với **bt tension compression tapping** technology.

### 4. Quick-Change Collet System – G3/G12/G24 Cover M3-M42

**BT tension compression tapping holder** uses 3-size collet system: 1× holder BT30/40/50 + 3× collets (G3/G12/G24) → cover entire M3-M42 metric thread range. Collet changeover time: 30 seconds (loosen collet nut → swap collet → tighten). Compare với dedicated tap holders: need 15-20× individual holders → **bt tension compression tapping holder** saves 12-17 ATC magazine slots. Cost saving: $300×15 = $4500 vs 1 holder $450 + 3 collets $50×3 = $600.

### 5. Compatible với Traditional & Rigid Tapping – Dual Functionality

**BT tension compression tapping holder** works với cả 2 tapping modes: (1) Traditional tapping (no spindle sync) - floating chuck fully compensates feed mismatch, prevents tap breakage. (2) Rigid tapping (G84 spindle sync) - floating chuck provides additional safety margin, absorbs minor CNC synchronization errors (<0.01mm). Nếu CNC không có G84 function → dùng traditional mode. Nếu CNC có G84 → dùng rigid mode for faster cycle time. **BT tension compression tapping** versatility means 1 holder works on any CNC machine type.
