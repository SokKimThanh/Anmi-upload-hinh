# BT-SLO Oil-Feed Side Lock Holder – Internal Coolant 4MPa, Side Lock Clamping, Φ6-Φ32

## BT-SLO Oil-Feed Side Lock Holder – Side Lock Type, Internal Coolant 4MPa, 7 Sizes

**BT-SLO oil-feed side lock holder** là giải pháp hàng đầu cho **internal coolant delivery** (coolant cấp qua holder) trong CNC machining operations yêu cầu cooling performance cao. Khác với external coolant (phun ngoài) chỉ làm mát bề mặt, **bt-slo oil-feed side lock holder** sử dụng **internal coolant channels** - coolant flow áp suất **≤4MPa** (40 bar) chạy thẳng qua holder body vào drill/cutter flutes, làm mát trực tiếp cutting zone, tăng tuổi thọ dao **70%** và chip evacuation tốt hơn **80%**.

Hệ thống **bt-slo oil-feed side lock holder** sử dụng **side lock clamping mechanism** - phương pháp kẹp dao bằng set screw từ bên hông holder. Side lock clamping có lợi thế **3-minute tool changeover** (không cần torque wrench như ER collet) và **universal shank compatibility** (accept bất kỳ tool shank diameter nào trong range). Hệ thống bao gồm **7 sizes**: SLO6 (Φ6mm), SLO8 (Φ8mm), SLO10 (Φ10mm), SLO12 (Φ12mm), SLO16 (Φ16mm), SLO20 (Φ20mm), SLO25 (Φ25mm), SLO32 (Φ32mm).

**Ứng dụng chính:** Deep hole drilling (L/D >5:1), high-speed milling titanium/Inconel, gun drilling operations, coolant-fed boring, aluminum high-speed machining (>20,000 RPM with coolant), stainless steel drilling with peck cycle coolant.

## 5 Tính Năng Vượt Trội Của BT-SLO Oil-Feed Side Lock Holder

1. **Internal Coolant 4MPa – Direct Cutting Zone Cooling:** **BT-SLO oil-feed side lock holder** có **internal coolant channels** integrated trong holder body - coolant flow từ spindle through-coolant connection → holder internal passages → tool shank coolant holes → cutting edges. Coolant pressure **≤4MPa** (40 bar) đủ để penetrate deep holes (L/D >10:1) và flush chips ra ngoài. Internal coolant tăng tool life **70%** so với external coolant (study: deep hole drilling Inconel 718, internal coolant 4MPa → 1500 holes tool life vs external coolant 900 holes). Coolant flow rate: 8-15 L/min tùy holder size.

2. **Side Lock Clamping – 3-Minute Tool Changeover:** **BT-SLO oil-feed side lock holder** sử dụng **side lock mechanism** - tool shank clamped bằng **set screw** (grub screw) từ bên hông holder. Side lock advantages: (1) **Quick changeover** - loosen 1 set screw → remove tool → insert new tool → tighten screw → total time **3 minutes** (vs ER collet 8 minutes: remove nut, change collet, tighten nut, torque check), (2) **No torque wrench** - hand tighten với hex key 15-20 Nm (vs ER collet need 100 Nm torque wrench), (3) **Universal shank** - accept any diameter trong range (ví dụ: SLO12 holder accept Φ10-12mm shanks).

3. **7 Sizes Cover Φ6-Φ32 – Full Drilling/Milling Range:** **BT-SLO oil-feed side lock holder** system bao gồm **7 holder sizes**: SLO6-BT40 (Φ6mm, micro drilling HSS/carbide drills), SLO8-BT40 (Φ8mm, small hole drilling), SLO10-BT40 (Φ10mm, common drilling size), SLO12-BT40 (Φ12mm, most popular), SLO16-BT40/50 (Φ16mm, medium drilling), SLO20-BT40/50 (Φ20mm, large drilling/milling), SLO25-BT50 (Φ25mm, heavy-duty), SLO32-BT50 (Φ32mm, extra-large drilling/boring). 7 sizes cover 95% drilling/milling applications trong general machining.

4. **Through-Spindle Coolant Compatible – CNC Integration:** **BT-SLO oil-feed side lock holder** được thiết kế cho **through-spindle coolant (TSC) systems** - CNC machining centers có coolant supply integrated trong spindle. TSC connection types: (1) **Rotary union** (coolant flow qua rotating spindle), (2) **Coolant pressure**: 2-4 MPa (20-40 bar), (3) **Flow rate**: 8-20 L/min tùy pump capacity. **BT-SLO holder** có O-ring seals tại taper và holder body để prevent coolant leakage. Compatible với major CNC brands: Mazak (Matrix system), DMG Mori (CELOS coolant), Haas (High-Pressure Coolant option), Okuma (Oscar coolant system).

5. **70% Tool Life Increase – Proven Performance Data:** **BT-SLO oil-feed side lock holder** với internal coolant tăng tool life **70%** average (data from 500+ case studies). Mechanism: (1) **Direct cooling** - coolant hits cutting edges immediately → cutting temperature giảm **40-60°C** → reduced tool wear, (2) **Chip evacuation** - high-pressure coolant flush chips out of hole → prevent re-cutting chips (major cause of tool failure), (3) **Lubrication** - coolant with oil additives reduce friction 30%. Example: drilling Φ12×50mm deep holes trong stainless steel SUS304 - external coolant: 180 holes/tool, **bt-slo internal coolant**: **320 holes/tool** → 78% improvement.

## Ứng Dụng Thực Tế BT-SLO Oil-Feed Side Lock Holder

### 1. Deep Hole Drilling (L/D >5:1) with Internal Coolant

**BT-SLO oil-feed side lock holder** là solution duy nhất cho **deep hole drilling** (length-to-diameter ratio >5:1) - operation khó khăn vì chip evacuation poor và heat buildup. Ví dụ: drilling Φ12×80mm deep holes (L/D = 6.7:1) trong carbon steel S45C. Setup: **bt-slo oil-feed side lock holder SLO12-BT40** + Φ12mm carbide drill (140° point, internal coolant Φ2mm holes) → coolant 3 MPa, flow 12 L/min (soluble oil 8% concentration) → drilling parameters: 1200 RPM (Vc=45 m/min), feed 0.08 mm/rev, peck cycle Q=3mm (retract 1mm every 3mm depth for chip breaking) → tool life **350 holes** per regrind. Compare with external coolant: tool life 180 holes → **94% improvement**.

### 2. High-Speed Milling Titanium (Ti-6Al-4V) with Coolant

**BT-SLO oil-feed side lock holder** critical cho **titanium milling** - material có very low thermal conductivity (heat không dissipate, concentrate tại cutting edges → rapid tool wear). Ví dụ: face milling titanium Ti-6Al-4V aerospace component. Setup: **BT-SLO oil-feed side lock holder SLO20-BT50** + Φ20mm indexable face mill (4 flutes, APKT inserts carbide grade for titanium) → internal coolant 4 MPa through inserts → milling parameters: 3000 RPM (Vc=188 m/min), feed 900 mm/min (0.075 mm/tooth), depth of cut 2mm, coolant flow 15 L/min (semi-synthetic coolant) → insert life **45 minutes** cutting time. Without internal coolant: insert life 18 minutes → **150% improvement**.

### 3. Gun Drilling Operations (L/D >10:1)

**BT-SLO oil-feed side lock holder** compatible với **gun drills** - specialized drills for very deep holes (L/D 10-100:1) commonly dùng trong automotive (crankshaft oil passages), mold & die (cooling channels). Ví dụ: gun drilling Φ8×120mm holes (L/D=15:1) trong alloy steel SCM440 (30 HRC). Setup: **bt-slo oil-feed side lock holder SLO8-BT40** + Φ8mm gun drill (single flute, carbide tip, V-shaped coolant channel) → coolant **4 MPa** (maximum pressure for gun drilling), flow 10 L/min (neat cutting oil for lubrication) → drilling parameters: 2000 RPM, feed 0.03 mm/rev (very slow feed for chip control), straight plunge (no peck cycle) → coolant pressure pushes chips backward through V-channel → tool life **80 holes** per regrind.

### 4. Stainless Steel Drilling with Peck Cycle Coolant

**BT-SLO oil-feed side lock holder** hiệu quả cho **stainless steel drilling** (SUS304, SUS316) - materials work-harden quickly (cần chip breaking và coolant intensive). Ví dụ: drilling Φ10×40mm holes trong SUS304 plate 50mm thick. Setup: **BT-SLO oil-feed side lock holder SLO10-BT40** + Φ10mm cobalt HSS drill (135° split point, internal coolant Φ1.5mm holes) → coolant 2.5 MPa, flow 10 L/min (water-soluble coolant 10% for stainless) → drilling parameters: 800 RPM (Vc=25 m/min slow speed prevent work hardening), feed 0.06 mm/rev, **peck cycle G83**: Q=2mm peck (retract full every 2mm to clear chips + coolant flush) → tool life **120 holes** per regrind (vs 45 holes external coolant).

### 5. Aluminum High-Speed Machining (>20,000 RPM)

**BT-SLO oil-feed side lock holder** cho **aluminum high-speed milling** với coolant prevents built-up edge (BUE) - aluminum's soft material tends to weld onto cutting edges. Ví dụ: high-speed milling aluminum 7075-T6 aerospace parts. Setup: **BT-SLO oil-feed side lock holder SLO12-BT40** + Φ12mm carbide end mill (2 flutes, high helix 45°, internal coolant through center) → coolant 2 MPa, flow 12 L/min (synthetic coolant + aluminum-specific additives) → milling parameters: **22,000 RPM** (Vc=830 m/min ultra-high speed), feed 4400 mm/min (0.1 mm/tooth), axial depth 6mm, radial 6mm (50% cutter diameter) → coolant prevents BUE, flush chips → tool life **8 hours** continuous cutting (vs 3 hours external coolant). ## Câu Hỏi Thường Gặp Về BT-SLO Oil-Feed Side Lock Holder

**BT-SLO oil-feed side lock holder có cần máy CNC đặc biệt không?**

Có. **BT-SLO oil-feed side lock holder** yêu cầu CNC machining center có **through-spindle coolant (TSC) system** - coolant supply integrated trong spindle với pump capacity 2-4 MPa. Check CNC specs cho: "TSC", "High-Pressure Coolant Option", "Internal Coolant System". Major CNC brands: Mazak (Matrix TSC standard on most Vertical Centers), DMG Mori (CELOS coolant package), Haas (High-Pressure Through-Spindle Coolant option ~$3000), Okuma (Oscar coolant system with rotary union). Nếu CNC không có TSC → không thể dùng **bt-slo holder** → consider [BT side lock holder standard](https://anmitools.com/san-pham/bt-side-lock-holder/) (no coolant) thay thế.

**Side lock clamping có bị loose trong high-speed operations không?**

Không, nếu tightening đúng. **BT-SLO oil-feed side lock holder** set screw (M6 or M8) yêu cầu torque **15-20 Nm** (hand tighten với hex key, check với torque wrench). Set screw tip có **flat tip** hoặc **cup point** (indent vào tool shank) → prevent rotation. Best practice: (1) Clean tool shank với acetone (remove oil/chips), (2) Insert tool, (3) Tighten set screw 15-20 Nm, (4) Run spindle 1000 RPM no load 30 seconds (test vibration), (5) Re-check torque. Nếu tool slip → check: (1) Set screw worn (replace), (2) Tool shank không round (measure với micrometer), (3) Torque insufficient. Tham khảo [Haimer tool clamping guide](https://www.haimer.com/en/products/tool-clamping-technology/) cho clamping best practices.

**BT-SLO holder run-out 0.02mm có ảnh hưởng hole quality không?**

Depends on operation tolerance: **Drilling** - hole tolerance H7 (±0.015mm for Φ12) → run-out 0.02mm = 133% tolerance → **marginal**. Solution: drill undersized Φ11.8 → ream to Φ12 H7 với precision ER collet holder (run-out <0.005mm). **Milling** - surface finish Ra 1.6μm → run-out 0.02mm acceptable (milling không sensitive bằng drilling). **Deep hole drilling** (L/D >5) → run-out 0.02mm causes hole drift 0.1-0.2mm at exit → acceptable cho clearance holes (không critical dimensions). Nếu cần precision holes H6 → dùng [BT-ER collet holder precision](https://anmitools.com/san-pham/bt-er-collet-holder/) thay thế.

**Coolant pressure 4 MPa có làm hỏng tool shank coolant holes không?**

Không, nếu tool designed đúng. Carbide drills/end mills có **internal coolant holes Φ1.5-3mm** được gun-drilled qua tool body → withstand pressure up to **10 MPa** (safety factor 2.5× for 4 MPa operating pressure). HSS tools (high-speed steel) có thinner walls → maximum pressure **3 MPa** (check tool manufacturer specs). Red flags: (1) Coolant leaking từ tool shank-holder interface → O-ring seals failed (replace), (2) Coolant flow decreased → coolant holes clogged (flush với high-pressure air), (3) Tool shank cracked → pressure too high hoặc tool defect. Always follow tool manufacturer's maximum coolant pressure ratings.

**BT-SLO holder có thể dùng với external coolant nozzles không?**

Có, nhưng mất internal coolant advantage. Nếu CNC không có through-spindle coolant, có thể dùng **bt-slo oil-feed side lock holder** như standard side lock holder (ignore internal coolant channels) + add external coolant nozzles aim tại cutting zone. Setup: 2-3 nozzles per tool, coolant pressure 0.5-1 MPa (low pressure flood coolant), flow rate 20-30 L/min. External coolant effectiveness: **30-40%** of internal coolant (coolant không reach cutting edges directly, chỉ cool bề mặt workpiece). Tool life improvement: external coolant +30-40% vs dry cutting (vs internal coolant +70%). Consider [BT-ERO oil-feed ER collet holder](https://anmitools.com/san-pham/bt-ero-oil-feed-er-collet/) cho precision operations với internal coolant.
