## NBJ16 Micro Boring System – 12 Boring Bars, Φ6-51mm Range, 0.01mm Precision

**NBJ16 micro boring** system là giải pháp boring bar compact chuyên dụng cho precision boring lỗ nhỏ-trung Φ6-51mm. Hệ thống bao gồm **12 boring bars** (SBJ-1606 → SBJ-1645) với **dial indicator 0.01mm/graduation** adjustment, đạt tolerance **IT7-IT8 (±0.012-0.018mm)** direct, không cần grinding. **NBJ16 micro boring** compact hơn NBH2084 (Φ8-280mm) → phù hợp cho máy CNC có tool magazine hạn chế, gia công bearing seat, mold cavity, hydraulic cylinder, precision mechanical parts trên holder BT40/BT50/NT40/SK40.

**Ứng dụng chính NBJ16 micro boring:** Bearing seat boring Φ6-51mm IT7 (H7 fit), mold cavity precision ±0.015mm, hydraulic cylinder sealing surface Ra 1.0μm, gearbox housing, pump body, valve body precision parts.

**Chọn insert type:**

- **CCMT060204:** Cho bars SBJ-1606~SBJ-1615 (Φ6-22mm)
- **TCMT110204:** Cho bars SBJ-1618~SBJ-1645 (Φ18-51mm)

**Lắp insert:**

1. **Đặt insert vào insert pocket** → đảm bảo insert seat contact 100%
2. **Siết clamp screw** (torque 2.5 Nm cho CCMT, 3.5 Nm cho TCMT)
3. **Check insert protrusion** → đảm bảo cutting edge không bị chìm vào body

### Bước 3: Điều Chỉnh Dial Indicator

**Adjustment process:**

1. **Rough adjust:** Xoay adjustment screw → đưa insert gần target diameter (±0.1mm)
2. **Dial reading:** Đọc dial indicator → xác định offset (ví dụ: +0.05mm)
3. **Fine adjust:** Xoay screw từng 0.01mm → đạt target ±0.005mm
4. **Lock screw:** Siết lock screw (torque 1.5 Nm) → cố định insert position
5. **Re-check:** Đo lại dial → confirm ±0.005mm accuracy

**Lưu ý:** Dial indicator graduation 0.01mm → chính xác ±0.005mm.

### Bước 4: Setup CNC Program

**Cutting parameters cho NBJ16 micro boring:**

**Steel (carbon steel, alloy steel):**
- **Speed:** 80-120 m/min (rough), 100-150 m/min (finish)
- **Feed:** 0.15-0.25 mm/rev (rough), 0.05-0.10 mm/rev (finish)
- **Depth of cut:** 0.5-1.5mm (rough), 0.1-0.3mm (finish)

**Cast iron (GG20, GG25, GG30):**
- **Speed:** 100-150 m/min (rough), 120-180 m/min (finish)
- **Feed:** 0.20-0.30 mm/rev (rough), 0.08-0.15 mm/rev (finish)

**Stainless steel (SUS304, SUS316):**
- **Speed:** 60-80 m/min (rough), 80-100 m/min (finish)
- **Feed:** 0.10-0.20 mm/rev (rough), 0.05-0.10 mm/rev (finish)
- **Depth of cut:** 0.3-1.0mm (rough), 0.1-0.3mm (finish)

### Bước 5: Boring Operation & Quality Check

**Boring operation:**

1. **Rough boring:** Remove material → leave 0.3-0.5mm stock for finish boring
2. **Semi-finish boring (optional):** For deep holes >100mm, add semi-finish pass → leave 0.1-0.2mm stock
3. **Finish boring:** Final pass → đạt target diameter ±0.005mm, Ra 0.8-1.0μm

**Quality check:**

1. **Diameter measurement:** Sử dụng internal micrometer hoặc bore gauge → confirm ±0.005mm accuracy
2. **Surface finish check:** Profilometer → confirm Ra 0.8-1.0μm (finish boring)
3. **Cylindricity check:** Bore gauge hoặc CMM → confirm ≤0.01mm/100mm length
