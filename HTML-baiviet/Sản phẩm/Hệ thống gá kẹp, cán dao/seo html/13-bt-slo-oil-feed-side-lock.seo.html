BT-SLO Oil-Feed Side Lock Holder – Side Lock Type, Internal Coolant 4MPa, 7 Sizes

BT-SLO oil-feed side lock holder là giải pháp hàng đầu cho internal coolant delivery (coolant cấp qua holder) trong CNC machining operations yêu cầu cooling performance cao. Khác với external coolant (phun ngoài) chỉ làm mát bề mặt, hệ thống này sử dụng internal coolant channels - coolant flow áp suất ≤4MPa (40 bar) chạy thẳng qua holder body vào drill/cutter flutes, làm mát trực tiếp cutting zone, tăng tuổi thọ dao 70% và chip evacuation tốt hơn 80%.

Hệ thống bt-slo oil-feed side lock holder sử dụng side lock clamping mechanism - phương pháp kẹp dao bằng set screw từ bên hông holder. Side lock clamping có lợi thế 3-minute tool changeover (không cần torque wrench như ER collet) và universal shank compatibility (accept bất kỳ tool shank diameter nào trong range). Hệ thống bao gồm 7 sizes: SLO6 (Φ6mm), SLO8 (Φ8mm), SLO10 (Φ10mm), SLO12 (Φ12mm), SLO16 (Φ16mm), SLO20 (Φ20mm), SLO25 (Φ25mm), SLO32 (Φ32mm).

Ứng dụng chính: Deep hole drilling (L/D >5:1), high-speed milling titanium/Inconel, gun drilling operations, coolant-fed boring, aluminum high-speed machining (>20,000 RPM with coolant), stainless steel drilling with peck cycle coolant.

  1. Internal Coolant 4MPa – Direct Cutting Zone Cooling: Hệ thống có internal coolant channels integrated trong holder body - coolant flow từ spindle through-coolant connection → holder internal passages → tool shank coolant holes → cutting edges. Coolant pressure ≤4MPa (40 bar) đủ để penetrate deep holes (L/D >10:1) và flush chips ra ngoài. Internal coolant tăng tool life 70% so với external coolant (study: deep hole drilling Inconel 718, internal coolant 4MPa → 1500 holes tool life vs external coolant 900 holes).   2. Side Lock Clamping – 3-Minute Tool Changeover: Sử dụng side lock mechanism - tool shank clamped bằng set screw (grub screw) từ bên hông holder. TSC connection types: (1) Rotary union (coolant flow qua rotating spindle), (2) Coolant pressure: 2-4 MPa (20-40 bar), (3) Flow rate: 8-20 L/min tùy pump capacity. Holder có O-ring seals tại taper và holder body để prevent coolant leakage. Compatible với major CNC brands: Mazak (Matrix system), DMG Mori (CELOS coolant), Haas (High-Pressure Coolant option), Okuma (Oscar coolant system).
  5. 70% Tool Life Increase – Proven Performance Data: Với internal coolant tăng tool life 70% average (data from 500+ case studies). Mechanism: (1) Direct cooling - coolant hits cutting edges immediately → cutting temperature giảm 40-60°C → reduced tool wear, (2) Chip evacuation - high-pressure coolant flush chips out of hole → prevent re-cutting chips (major cause of tool failure), (3) Lubrication - coolant with oil additives reduce friction 30%. Example: drilling Φ12×50mm deep holes trong stainless steel SUS304 - external coolant: 180 holes/tool, internal coolant: 320 holes/tool → 78% improvement.

Ứng Dụng Thực Tế BT-SLO Oil-Feed Side Lock Holder

1. Deep Hole Drilling (L/D >5:1) with Internal Coolant

Là solution duy nhất cho deep hole drilling (length-to-diameter ratio >5:1) - operation khó khăn vì chip evacuation poor và heat buildup. Ví dụ: drilling Φ12×80mm deep holes (L/D = 6.7:1) trong carbon steel S45C. Setup: SLO12-BT40 + Φ12mm carbide drill (140° point, internal coolant Φ2mm holes) → coolant 3 MPa, flow 12 L/min (soluble oil 8% concentration) → drilling parameters: 1200 RPM (Vc=45 m/min), feed 0.08 mm/rev, peck cycle Q=3mm (retract 1mm every 3mm depth for chip breaking) → tool life 350 holes per regrind. Compare with external coolant: tool life 180 holes → 94% improvement.

2. High-Speed Milling Titanium (Ti-6Al-4V) with Coolant

Critical cho titanium milling - material có very low thermal conductivity (heat không dissipate, concentrate tại cutting edges → rapid tool wear). Ví dụ: face milling titanium Ti-6Al-4V aerospace component. Setup: SLO20-BT50 + Φ20mm indexable face mill (4 flutes, APKT inserts carbide grade for titanium) → internal coolant 4 MPa through inserts → milling parameters: 3000 RPM (Vc=188 m/min), feed 900 mm/min (0.075 mm/tooth), depth of cut 2mm, coolant flow 15 L/min (semi-synthetic coolant) → insert life 45 minutes cutting time. Without internal coolant: insert life 18 minutes → 150% improvement.

3. Gun Drilling Operations (L/D >10:1)

Compatible với gun drills - specialized drills for very deep holes (L/D 10-100:1) commonly dùng trong automotive (crankshaft oil passages), mold & die (cooling channels). Ví dụ: gun drilling Φ8×120mm holes (L/D=15:1) trong alloy steel SCM440 (30 HRC). Setup: SLO8-BT40 + Φ8mm gun drill (single flute, carbide tip, V-shaped coolant channel) → coolant 4 MPa (maximum pressure for gun drilling), flow 10 L/min (neat cutting oil for lubrication) → drilling parameters: 2000 RPM, feed 0.03 mm/rev (very slow feed for chip control), straight plunge (no peck cycle) → coolant pressure pushes chips backward through V-channel → tool life 80 holes per regrind.

4. Stainless Steel Drilling with Peck Cycle Coolant

Hiệu quả cho stainless steel drilling (SUS304, SUS316) - materials work-harden quickly (cần chip breaking và coolant intensive). Ví dụ: drilling Φ10×40mm holes trong SUS304 plate 50mm thick. Setup: SLO10-BT40 + Φ10mm cobalt HSS drill (135° split point, internal coolant Φ1.5mm holes) → coolant 2.5 MPa, flow 10 L/min (water-soluble coolant 10% for stainless) → drilling parameters: 800 RPM (Vc=25 m/min slow speed prevent work hardening), feed 0.06 mm/rev, peck cycle G83: Q=2mm peck (retract full every 2mm to clear chips + coolant flush) → tool life 120 holes per regrind (vs 45 holes external coolant).

5. Aluminum High-Speed Machining (>20,000 RPM)

Cho aluminum high-speed milling với coolant prevents built-up edge (BUE) - aluminum's soft material tends to weld onto cutting edges. Ví dụ: high-speed milling aluminum 7075-T6 aerospace parts. Setup: SLO12-BT40 + Φ12mm carbide end mill (2 flutes, high helix 45°, internal coolant through center) → coolant 2 MPa, flow 12 L/min (synthetic coolant + aluminum-specific additives) → milling parameters: 22,000 RPM (Vc=830 m/min ultra-high speed), feed 4400 mm/min (0.1 mm/tooth), axial depth 6mm, radial 6mm (50% cutter diameter) → coolant prevents BUE, flush chips → tool life 8 hours continuous cutting (vs 3 hours external coolant). Câu Hỏi Thường Gặp Về BT-SLO Oil-Feed Side Lock Holder

BT-SLO oil-feed side lock holder có cần máy CNC đặc biệt không? +

Có. Hệ thống yêu cầu CNC machining center có through-spindle coolant (TSC) system - coolant supply integrated trong spindle với pump capacity 2-4 MPa. Check CNC specs cho: "TSC", "High-Pressure Coolant Option", "Internal Coolant System". Major CNC brands: Mazak (Matrix TSC standard on most Vertical Centers), DMG Mori (CELOS coolant package), Haas (High-Pressure Through-Spindle Coolant option ~$3000), Okuma (Oscar coolant system with rotary union). Nếu CNC không có TSC → không thể dùng → consider BT side lock holder standard (no coolant) thay thế.

Side lock clamping có bị loose trong high-speed operations không? +

Không, nếu tightening đúng. Set screw (M6 or M8) yêu cầu torque 15-20 Nm (hand tighten với hex key, check với torque wrench). Set screw tip có flat tip hoặc cup point (indent vào tool shank) → prevent rotation. Best practice: (1) Clean tool shank với acetone (remove oil/chips), (2) Insert tool, (3) Tighten set screw 15-20 Nm, (4) Run spindle 1000 RPM no load 30 seconds (test vibration), (5) Re-check torque. Nếu tool slip → check: (1) Set screw worn (replace), (2) Tool shank không round (measure với micrometer), (3) Torque insufficient. Tham khảo Haimer tool clamping guide cho clamping best practices.

BT-SLO holder run-out 0.02mm có ảnh hưởng hole quality không? +

Depends on operation tolerance: Drilling - hole tolerance H7 (±0.015mm for Φ12) → run-out 0.02mm = 133% tolerance → marginal. Solution: drill undersized Φ11.8 → ream to Φ12 H7 với precision ER collet holder (run-out <0.005mm). Milling - surface finish Ra 1.6μm → run-out 0.02mm acceptable (milling không sensitive bằng drilling). Deep hole drilling (L/D >5) → run-out 0.02mm causes hole drift 0.1-0.2mm at exit → acceptable cho clearance holes (không critical dimensions). Nếu cần precision holes H6 → dùng BT-ER collet holder precision thay thế.

Coolant pressure 4 MPa có làm hỏng tool shank coolant holes không? +

Không, nếu tool designed đúng. Carbide drills/end mills có internal coolant holes Φ1.5-3mm được gun-drilled qua tool body → withstand pressure up to 10 MPa (safety factor 2.5× for 4 MPa operating pressure). HSS tools (high-speed steel) có thinner walls → maximum pressure 3 MPa (check tool manufacturer specs). Red flags: (1) Coolant leaking từ tool shank-holder interface → O-ring seals failed (replace), (2) Coolant flow decreased → coolant holes clogged (flush với high-pressure air), (3) Tool shank cracked → pressure too high hoặc tool defect. Always follow tool manufacturer's maximum coolant pressure ratings.

BT-SLO holder có thể dùng với external coolant nozzles không? +

Có, nhưng mất internal coolant advantage. Nếu CNC không có through-spindle coolant, có thể dùng như standard side lock holder (ignore internal coolant channels) + add external coolant nozzles aim tại cutting zone. Setup: 2-3 nozzles per tool, coolant pressure 0.5-1 MPa (low pressure flood coolant), flow rate 20-30 L/min. External coolant effectiveness: 30-40% of internal coolant (coolant không reach cutting edges directly, chỉ cool bề mặt workpiece). Tool life improvement: external coolant +30-40% vs dry cutting (vs internal coolant +70%). Consider BT-ERO oil-feed ER collet holder cho precision operations với internal coolant.
