BT-SLO Oil-Feed Side Lock Holder – Internal Coolant 4MPa, Side Lock Clamping Φ6–Φ32

BT-SLO Oil-Feed Side Lock Holder – Side Lock Type, Internal Coolant 4MPa, 7 Sizes

BT-SLO oil-feed side lock holder là giải pháp hàng đầu cho internal coolant delivery trong CNC machining operations yêu cầu cooling performance cao. Internal coolant channels (pressure ≤4MPa) chạy thẳng qua holder body vào tool flutes, tăng tuổi thọ dao và cải thiện chip evacuation.

5 Tính Năng Vượt Trội

  1. Internal Coolant 4MPa – Direct Cutting Zone Cooling: Internal coolant passages deliver coolant directly to cutting edges, improving tool life and chip evacuation.
  2. Side Lock Clamping – 3-Minute Changeover: Quick changeover using a single set screw; no torque wrench required.
  3. 7 Sizes Φ6–Φ32: Covers most drilling and milling needs across general machining.
  4. TSC Compatible: Designed to integrate with through-spindle coolant systems and O-ring seals to prevent leakage.
  5. Proven Tool Life Gains: Case studies show average tool life improvements of ~70% vs external coolant.
