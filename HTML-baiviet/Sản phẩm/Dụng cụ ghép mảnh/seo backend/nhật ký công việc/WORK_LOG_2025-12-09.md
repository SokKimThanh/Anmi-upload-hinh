## 3. Terminology Clarification

### 3.1. Đầu Phay vs Dao Phay
**User question**: "đầu phay với dao phay giống hay khác nhau?"

**Answer provided**:
- **Đầu phay (Milling Head/Cutter Body)**: Component only, needs assembly
  - Example: FM901F head needs shank + insert
- **Dao phay (Milling Cutter/Tool)**: Complete assembly
  - Example: FM901F head + modular shank + BXKT11T3 insert

**Application to FM901F**:
- Product is technically "đầu phay" (modular head component)
- Marketing term "dao phay" also acceptable (implies complete system)
- Used "Đầu Phay Vai Vuông FM901F" for technical accuracy
